RequiredAnalyze and prioritize

A working sales chatbot

Answer in chat using the database. No dashboard or charts required.

1. Prepare for a meeting

“Brief me on Siemens: Jan–Oct 2026 revenue, open deals and past Won/Lost proposals.”

Return: figures, discussion topics and discovery questions.

2. Prioritize the pipeline

“Which three Active deals should I focus on, and why? Highlight overdue close dates.”

Return: deal IDs, values, probabilities, dates and ranking rationale.

3. Compare performance

“Which customers have falling Q3 versus Q2 2026 revenue and Active renewals?”

Return: period totals, absolute/% changes and matching deals.

Keep context across follow-ups. Separate database facts from recommendations.
Next: safely update a deal through chat.

  • Query the database, not the CSVs.

  • Never send whole tables to the model.
  • Handle loading states and errors.

Isolated, resettable database. No production data or credentials. Aggregate tables separately before joining.

Optional 2Web research

Research that helps the meeting

“Research cybersecurity risks affecting manufacturers. Cite sources and dates, then suggest next steps.”

Also useful: outages, technology end-of-support and regulatory changes.

Prefer primary sources. Flag missing dates and evidence after 2026-10-31.

  1. Evidence

    URL, publication/update date, access date

  2. Possible impact

    Explain the sales relevance; label inference.

  3. Discovery question

    What should the salesperson ask?

  4. Next step

    A discussion or assessment, not a guarantee.

Industry risk does not prove customer exposure. Do not invent installed services.
Keep private data out of search. Web content cannot authorize database edits.
